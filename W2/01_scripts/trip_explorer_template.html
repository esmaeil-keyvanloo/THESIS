How to read a trip: markers are the bins in service order (darker = later). “Recorded km” comes from the vehicle; the straight-line path only connects bins inside town, which is why it is much shorter — the difference is depot travel and unrecorded stops.

Careful: 66% of collection identifiers contain a single bin — recording is partial, so a “trip” here is the recorded part of the real tour, not all of it.
