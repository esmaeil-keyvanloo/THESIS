Reading a trip: the line is the shortest road path visiting the recorded bins in order — the real driven route will differ where the crew took detours or served unrecorded bins (66% of identifiers record a single bin). Weight is the weighbridge total for the whole run (bin capacity 2500 is litres, not kg — weight is never derived from fill). "Recorded km" and load come from the vehicle identifier; when an identifier was split into tracks they are totals shared by all its tracks. A segment is only drawn when the truck could physically drive it (≤60 km/h implied); faster jumps mean a second vehicle was recording under the same identifier, so those stops form their own track.
